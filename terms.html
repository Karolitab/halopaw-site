What HaloPaw is

HaloPaw is a gentle place to remember a pet. From the photos you choose, we make a pixel drawing of your pet (their "pixel twin"). Your pet then lives on their own island in the app, with a room, a memory book and care you can give them each day.

The pixel twin is drawn by an AI art service. It is an artist's likeness, not an exact copy, and it can get details wrong. If it does not look like your pet, tell us and we will try to help.

Free and paid

Each phone gets one free pixel twin. Without a subscription your pet stands on their island and the memory book is open to you.

HaloPaw Pro unlocks the rest: your pet's animations and moves, feeding, brushing and playing, their room and the shop. The prices are shown in the app before you pay, in your local currency where available.
