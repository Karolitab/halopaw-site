HaloPaw

Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

In short

  • HaloPaw has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We never sell your data.
  • Almost everything stays on your phone: your pet's details, your memory book and your photos.
  • The photos you choose leave your phone for one reason: so we can draw your pet in pixels.
  • Apple handles payments. We never see your card or your Apple ID.

Who we are

HaloPaw is made by Tim Gabe Holding Sociedad Limitada, a company in Spain. We are responsible for the data described here. You can reach us at halopawapp@yahoo.com.

What stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Your answers about your pet: whether they were a boy or a girl, their personality and their birthday.
  • Your memory book: the memories you write and the stickers you add.
  • The picture you put in your pet's room, and your care progress (hearts, happiness and the items you unlock).
  • Copies of the photos you picked and of your pet's pixel drawings.

HaloPaw only sees the photos you choose in the photo picker. It cannot see the rest of your library, your contacts, your location or your camera.

What we receive and why

To draw your pet

When you finish the questions, the app sends up to three of the photos you chose, your pet's name and whether they were a dog, a cat or another animal to our server. Our server puts the photos side by side and sends them to SpriteCook, the pixel art service that draws your pet. Our server does not keep the photos after this. SpriteCook keeps that reference picture and the drawings it makes in our SpriteCook account.

To keep your drawings and stop abuse

The app makes a random ID the first time it talks to our server and keeps it on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising ID. We use it to give each phone one free drawing, and to send you back the same drawing if you ask again instead of charging for a new one. We keep your pet's drawings on our server, linked to this ID and to a fingerprint of the drawing.

To check your subscription

Payments go through Apple. When you subscribe, the app sends our server the proof of purchase Apple signs for your subscription. We check it with Apple and keep its transaction number, so each subscription makes one set of animations for one pet. We never receive your card details or who you are.

To keep the service running

Our server writes short logs for each request: the time, whether it worked, how long it took and the first eight characters of the random app ID. We use them only to find and fix problems.

Who helps us

  • SpriteCook draws your pet from your photos.
  • Our cloud hosting provider runs our server and stores your pet's drawings.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payments under its own privacy policy.

These services may store data outside the European Union. When they do, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

What we never do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics tools or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How long we keep it

What is on your phone stays until you delete the app. Your pet's drawings and the random app ID stay on our server so you can get them back, until you ask us to delete them or we close the service. Logs are deleted once they are no longer needed to fix problems.

Your choices and rights

You can delete everything on your phone by deleting the app. To have your pet's drawings deleted from our server and from SpriteCook, email us at halopawapp@yahoo.com with your pet's name and roughly when you made it, so we can find it.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or object to it. We use your data to provide the service you ask for, and to prevent misuse of the free drawing, which is our legitimate interest. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Spain that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

You can cancel your subscription anytime in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Children

HaloPaw is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has used HaloPaw, email us and we will delete their drawings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
